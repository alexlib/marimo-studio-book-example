A live book, in three chapters

The Spectral Lens

Eigenvalues, graphs, and the structure they reveal — every figure on every page is computed live, in your browser, by a Python notebook.

  1. 1

    The Shape of a Matrix

    A symmetric matrix stretches the plane along its eigenvectors. The eigendecomposition M = VDVT, made physical.

    You can touch: the matrix itself, its eigenvalues, and a lasso-selectable vector field.

  2. 2

    Drawing with Eigenvectors

    A graph has no coordinates. The eigenvectors of its Laplacian invent coordinates that respect its connectivity.

    You can touch: the graph family, its size, and which eigenvectors serve as the x- and y-axes.

  3. 3

    Filtering on Graphs

    The Laplacian eigenbasis is a Fourier basis for graphs. Project a noisy signal onto its smooth end and the noise falls away.

    You can touch: the signal, the noise level, and the filter cutoff — with live mean-squared-error scoring.

How this book works

Each chapter is an ordinary marimo notebook from the marimo gallery. marimo studio projects named cells of each notebook into a hand-designed web page: tags like <marimo-cell name="spectral_layout"> pull in live figures, and the sliders you drag re-run the same cells a reader could inspect in the notebook.

Unlike a rendered notebook, the page owns its presentation — the sidebar, the margin notes, the order of ideas — while the notebook stays the single source of the computation. Each chapter ships as a static WebAssembly bundle, so the Python you are running was installed by your browser, not a server.
